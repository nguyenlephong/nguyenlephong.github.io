## Core beliefs

### Engineering

1. **Ship small and real.** A finished, imperfect thing beats a perfect plan. "A finished, imperfect thing teaches you more than a perfect thing you never ship." (`letter-to-my-20s`). "Think just enough to act, then act, then think again with what you learned." (`thinking-and-doing-rhythm`)
2. **Start with a token step.** Momentum comes from the first concrete move: "write one line of code, send the email, open the document" (`the-cost-of-delayed-action`). "A tiny push that actually happens beats a perfect plan that never leaves the page." (`the-physics-laws-of-life`)
3. **Direct code over clever code.** "Every abstraction is a bet", and you can always abstract later (`programming-abstractions`). He wants anyone to "Ctrl/Cmd+Click and simulate how the runtime would unfold" (same note). Readers include "you, a couple of months from now".
4. **Write for the next reader.** Names and comments are beacons for the next person (`programming-beacons`). With AI writing code, the human-readable layer moved up to "the prompt, the spec, the architectural decision" that must "still be legible six months from now" (`code-run-on-people`).
5. **Upkeep is the real work.** "Left alone, things fall apart. Relationships, codebases, habits, health." "The upkeep, not the launch, is where the real work lives." (`the-physics-laws-of-life`). "A codebase is a living thing" (`programming-abstractions`).
6. **Tools are held loosely.** "Adopt the function; stay free of the tool." (`anchoring-on-tool-capability-limits-the-mind`). "Stop trying to choose the best of, and start trying to make the most out of." (`perfect-tools-syndrome`)
7. **Writing is how thinking finishes.** "If you cannot put an idea into words ... the thought was never concluded." (`articulation-skill`). "I don't believe someone can be a good thinker and a bad writer." (`what-if-i-found-a-startup`)
8. **AI moves the edge to framing.** "The facts have become a commodity; the framing is the edge." (`ai-reduces-the-information-privilege`)

### Decisions

9. **Decide, then commit.** "The real skill is not choosing perfectly, but choosing quickly and moving on." (`indecisiveness`). Deciding and doing are separate modes; judge the move later "with a calm head. Not mid-stride." (`modal-thinking`)
10. **Own decisions made with little information.** "Sometimes you make a decision with little information at hand, and you take responsibility for it." (`letter-to-my-20s`)
11. **Reversible fast, irreversible together.** Type 1 vs Type 2 decisions; "Disagree and commit"; "Data-informed, not data-driven" (`what-if-i-found-a-startup`). This is the same instinct as feature flags and kill switches on the profile.

### People and teams

12. **Trust by default, verify what drives a decision.** "Working with people: I trust them by default." Also: "Don't follow a process arrogantly" and he does not trust information that drives a decision "without validating it first" (`trust`).
13. **Mistakes must be survivable.** "Tolerate imperfect attempts. Leave room for others and for yourself to learn. A space where mistakes are survivable is a space where people actually grow." (`letter-to-my-20s`)
14. **Ego is not the driver.** "Your ego is not you. The voice defending your image is a passenger, not the driver." (`letter-to-my-20s`). A leader "does not have to be the best coder" but must hold the direction (`duong-tang-giu-vision-va-trao-quyen`).
15. **Agreement is not proof.** "One hundred percent agreement can mean the people ... don't dare to speak up." A design review where everyone signs off in minutes "means the work was never truly examined." (`healthy-disagreement`)
16. **Talent needs a track, not a cage.** Boundaries say "within this space you can decide; beyond this space, others are affected" (`ton-ngo-khong-nhan-tai-10x-va-vong-kim-co`). "No brilliant jerks: competence never excuses poor collaboration." (`what-if-i-found-a-startup`)
17. **Agency and ownership.** He wants people who "spot the problem before I do, then solve it without being asked", small pods "with full ownership", leadership that rotates "by expertise" (`what-if-i-found-a-startup`).
18. **Credit by evidence.** Each person keeps a "glory list" of wins, reviewed together in 1:1s (`what-if-i-found-a-startup`). Same idea as per-person velocity on /about: credit goes to the right people.
19. **Honest updates under pressure.** Separate "what is known from what is estimated and what is still unknown". "Friday if the interface stays unchanged" beats "Friday is guaranteed" (`tao-niem-tin-khong-phai-tao-ao-giac`).
20. **Character shows under load.** "Character is proven in wars, not in peace." (`letter-to-my-20s`)

## How he leads

- **Attention goes to** where each person stands and what they want, real strengths over titles (`duong-tang-giu-vision-va-trao-quyen`: "Assigning work without respecting real strengths is a fast way to make a good person look weak"), quiet roles that only show when missing (`bat-gioi-sa-tang-bach-long-ma-nhung-vai-tham-lang-trong-team`), and whether the room actually disagrees.
- **Change:** take a small real step, then adjust. Write rules down once so they "can simply run" (`life-running-digital-engine`). Systems over willpower: "build the scaffolding that carries you when both run out" (`letter-to-my-20s`). On a team that means the playbook, so decisions do not wait for one person (About.story.handover.shift).
- **Pressure:** honest status, visible limits, repair before anyone has to chase him (`tao-niem-tin-khong-phai-tao-ao-giac`). He does not hand out false calm.
- **Trust:** given first, kept through small kept promises. Example he admires: "take care of your family first; I will move the two urgent tickets" (`luu-bi-nhan-tri-noi-cong-so`).
- **Growth of others:** stretch "just slightly above" current capacity (`the-physics-laws-of-life`), allow imperfect attempts, plan growth with each person, measure with numbers they can see.
- **Himself:** admits limits plainly. "I still catch myself doing it." (`thinking-excuses`). "Some days I ask myself whether I am steady enough to stand in that caption. The honest answer is not entirely." and "I am not doing this alone." (`the-quiet-engineering-behind-bonbons-500k-round`). He still opens the terminal at night to read sync logs (same note).

## Natural voice

### English

- Short declaratives, often a short sentence after a longer one: "Thinking without acting is imagination. Acting without thinking is impulse." (`thinking-and-doing-rhythm`)
- Plain engineering nouns: repo, test suite, rollout, sprint, 1:1, spec, decision record, on-call.
- Admits limits in first person, without drama: "I still catch myself doing it."
- Uses "we" for team results, "I" for his own decision or code.
- Heuristics stated as rules of thumb: "Trust by default." "Ship daily." "Disagree and commit."
- Would say: shipped, led, wrote, built, set up, took ownership, rolled out, rolled back, trust, credit, the team.
- Would not say: passionate, empowered, drove alignment, synergy, journey (about himself), "my leadership", "I transformed".

### Vietnamese

- Câu ngắn, chủ ngữ rõ, nói như dev trao đổi trên Slack. Mẫu tốt: "Ok, mình đang vào họp; 2 giờ mình xem kỹ rồi phản hồi" (`work-messages-aggressive-bias`), "Ai cũng có lý." (`duong-tang-giu-vision-va-trao-quyen`), "Chừng đó đủ để mọi người tính đường." (`tao-niem-tin-khong-phai-tao-ao-giac`).
- Gọi người trong team là "từng bạn", "mỗi bạn"; team là "team", không "đội ngũ nhân sự".
- Giữ thuật ngữ nghề bằng tiếng Anh như người trong nghề nói: sprint, rollout, rollback, 1:1, repo, tenant, velocity.
- Thừa nhận giới hạn thẳng: "Câu trả lời thật lòng là chưa hoàn toàn." "Mình không đi một mình." (BonBon note, VI)
- Dùng: dẫn, làm, đưa tới người dùng, nhận phụ trách, cùng team, ghi nhận đúng người, dễ thở hơn.
- Không dùng: kiến tạo, dẫn dắt sự chuyển đổi, truyền cảm hứng, tầm nhìn chiến lược, đột phá, lặng lẽ, thấu cảm, "tôi đã thay đổi toàn bộ".

## What "leader vibe" means for him

- **Confident:** the number or the shipped thing carries the weight. No hedging, no superlatives.
- **Impact first:** the line opens with what users or the team got (back on sprint pace, 80% finished the flow, a working product in four weeks), then how.
- **People first:** the team is in the sentence. He names what people needed (to know where they stand, to be credited fairly), not the ceremony he ran.
- **No ego:** he holds direction and lets capable people own the route (`duong-tang-giu-vision-va-trao-quyen`). He is the one who should not become the bottleneck. Never "I rewrote", "I redefined", "I saved the team".
- **Honest about scope:** part-time is part-time, "led" when shared, "wrote" only for his own code, and limits are stated before someone asks.

## Checklist for profile copy in his voice

- [ ] The first clause is the result for users or the team, not the process.
- [ ] People appear in the sentence ("the team", "each person", "we"), and credit matches the real share.
- [ ] At most one mechanism per line. No list of rituals (1:1s, plans, metrics) without saying what they were for.
- [ ] No ego verbs: transformed, redefined, rewrote everything, saved, single-handedly, spearheaded.
- [ ] None of the tic words above; zero em dashes; no "not X but Y".
- [ ] One plain stance at most, traceable to a belief in this file (trust by default, small reversible steps, direction over doing everything).
- [ ] EN reads like his notes: short sentence after a long one, concrete nouns.
- [ ] VI written fresh with "tôi", no comma before "và", reads aloud like a Vietnamese engineer talking.
- [ ] Every fact and number is in the evidence ledger and matches every other surface.
